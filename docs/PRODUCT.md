# Product

**FLAT//RELAY**: send $WIF as something to play, not just a transfer.

- **Creators** (KOLs, Discord mods, Robinhood Chain projects, friends) fund a campaign and share a link.
- **Players** play a 20–45 s skill game, then claim $WIF gasless to any address.
- **Everyone** sees the public proof page and relay map built from onchain events.

## Modes
- **Gift:** private link, key in the URL fragment, no server.
- **Open challenge:** public link, verifier replays the game.

## Flywheel
Creator funds (1% burned) → link shared → players win $WIF (new holders) → winners re-gift → the map draws more creators.

## Categories
Games · Social and Creators · Consumer and Payments · Onboarding (only if gasless paste-address claim ships).

## Out of scope
Entry fees, new tokens, admin withdrawals, LLM runtime, multi-chain, vote-linked rewards.
